# AI-assisted contributions

AI assistance is welcome. You are responsible for everything you submit,
whether you wrote it yourself or used an AI tool.

## Review before submitting

Before submitting an issue or pull request:

- Personally review the content and verify its claims.
- Understand the proposed changes and be able to explain them.
- Run the relevant checks for code changes and report the results, including
  failures or checks you could not run.
- Be prepared to address maintainer feedback.

AI-generated explanations and passing tests do not replace human review.
A human maintainer must review a pull request and its verification evidence
before merging it.

## No fully automated submissions

A human must submit each issue or pull request. AI tools may help draft the
content and prepare changes, but must not submit them on your behalf.

Maintainers may close automated, spam, or low-effort submissions without
further discussion. Repeated abuse, including batches of automated or
low-effort submissions, may result in restrictions on further contributions.

AI-assisted contributions must follow the same contribution requirements
as any other submission. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
