sources, including designated package registries. Commercial coverage under this
agreement is limited to stable releases. Prerelease versions are not covered by
either commercial plan. Keeping an unchanged private mirror or fork does not
itself modify the software.

**Major line:** releases sharing a major version, such as `1.x`.
A perpetual grant for `1.x` includes its existing and future stable minor and
patch releases, including `1.4.x` and `1.5.x`, but not `2.x` unless a separate

**Minor line:** releases sharing a major and minor version, such as `1.4.x`.
Minor lines determine shared LTS windows, not the scope of a perpetual purchase.

**Customer modifications:** customer changes to Software code, including additions,
removals, and subsystem changes. Ordinary installation, bundling, tree-shaking,
minification, and transpilation do not alone require a commercial purchase.
This description does not redefine Modifications under MPL.

**Customer product:** a genuinely different application, framework, SDK, or other
product integrating Software, rather than standalone Software or a substantially
equivalent derivative offered as a separate library.

**LTS window:** twelve months from a minor line's initial stable release date.
Every customer shares the line's published dates. Purchases, renewals, and
replacement grants do not reset them.
The project publishes each line's start and end dates with the changelog for its
initial stable release through the official distribution channels.

**Qualifying defect:** either a reproducible bug in unmodified official Software
that violates documented behavior, or a reproducible security vulnerability in
unmodified official Software, whether or not it violates an express documentation
promise. A private-fork customer qualifies when the underlying defect reproduces
in the official release. Feature requests, customer-specific integration work,
and defects introduced solely by private changes are excluded.

One recurring purchase covers one customer across all stable Software releases

### 4.2. Perpetual

A one-time purchase covers one specified major line, including all its existing
and future stable minor and patch releases. The customer may continue modifying and
distributing that line in customer products without renewal payments, subject
to this agreement.
Perpetual means no ordinary time-based expiry, not immunity from refund or
breach provisions.

Two distinct major lines require two perpetual purchases unless an express
replacement grant supplies the additional line. The purchase does not cover
other major lines. Later stable minor and patch releases within the covered
major line require no additional purchase.

Maintenance follows each stable minor line's own shared twelve-month LTS window,
with the outstanding-report protection in section 7. Buying a major line does
not restart existing minor-line windows. A later stable minor line within the
covered major has its own window from its initial stable release. Coverage does
not promise that future releases will be produced or extend a minor line's LTS.

For a remediable breach, the licensor gives written notice identifying it and

period, the licensor may terminate the affected commercial license.

Otherwise-authorized distribution may continue during cure while the breach is
corrected. Offending conduct is not authorized: prohibited standalone resale
must stop rather than continue for another 90 days.

Cure neither extends a subscription nor postpones an effective refund.
Enforcement strategy is case-specific; no mandatory sequence of negotiations
or litigation is promised. No additional immediate breach-termination exception
overrides the 90-day cure period. Refund, chargeback, and ordinary expiry rules
remain distinct from termination for an uncured breach.

Upon termination for an uncured breach, further distribution relying on the
commercial grant must stop immediately. Already lawfully distributed software
may continue operating; the customer is not required to remove or disable copies
on end-user devices. This survival does not permit new deliveries of private
modifications, including through browser bundles, CDNs, or downloads. Independent
MPL-compliant distribution remains permitted. The application-sharing survival
expressly granted for expiry and refund is not extended here to breach termination.

Termination does not prevent the customer from reporting issues in official
Software. Termination ends the affected purchase's contractual maintenance and
fix obligations, including for reports received before termination. Subject to
mandatory legal obligations, the project may still evaluate reports of issues
reproducible in the unmodified official Software codebase, including security
reports and reports submitted after termination. Reproduction must not depend
on the customer's custom changes. This discretionary evaluation creates no
contractual guarantee of a fix, reinstates no terminated commercial grant, and
authorizes no further proprietary distribution. Mandatory legal obligations and
entitlements under other independently valid purchases remain unaffected.
